It is a **human, first-person letter**, not a pitch template. As of v4 it was completely rebuilt away from the lulubug-style audit format after Jacob's feedback: "it seems really ai'y and not human to human... change it away from a template."

**HARD lessons, do not regress:**
- NO audit of Ignite's clients' sites. Jon Bowling's site is a locked vendor template (Inside Real Estate) he can't change; NITRO/UNDIVIDED may be dead projects; Jacob is unsure whether Sean built the Creamalicious site or whether its imagery is Sean's own AI marketing. Auditing ANY of it risks insulting the person Jacob wants to befriend. All of it was removed.
- NO splash gate, nav, section cards, "In one look" grid, "The Pattern", "The Worked Example". Those are gone. It is a flowing letter.
- The ONLY external artifact is the Creamalicious welcome email Jacob actually designed (`images/creamalicious-email.jpg`, his real file from Downloads). Framed as enthusiasm ("got carried away and designed this"), never as critique of their site.
- Voice: genuine, specific, conversational, first person, slightly imperfect. Real details (the Walmart parking lot, his dad filming, Creamalicious, Cincinnati). No aphorisms, no punchy fragments, no "zero overlap" cadence.
- Zero em dashes. No AI vocab. Aurean strictly past tense. No dollar amounts. Never name The Katalyst Group / Sean Sr / the family tie in writing.
