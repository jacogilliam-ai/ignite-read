The pattern

Attention needs somewhere to land.

None of the builds below are your work. That is exactly the point. The websites came before you, the analytics were someone else's job years ago, and nobody owns the lane between your videos and a result you can measure. Three quick examples, all from your own roster.

01

Jon Bowling

The domain he owns is a 1990s-style HTML frameset wrapping the RE/MAX corporate template. No analytics, no email capture, no link previews on it. Every lead your videos send there lands somewhere no one can measure.

02

NITRO

The event page now returns "page not found." The parent site still runs Universal Analytics, which Google shut off in 2023. The content ran, and nothing recorded it.

03

UNDIVIDED

The site's Google search description is the placeholder text "Begins with U." It shipped, and no one ever went back to finish the line.

Nobody owns this lane for your clients. That is the opening.

The worked example

I went deep on one: your flagship.

SoCreamalicious.co, every finding re-checked today. All of it predates you. The site was built long before your videos, on tools that have since been pulled out from under it.

01

The shop page shows raw code where products should be.

The /shop/ page renders stray lines of code instead of ice cream. The reason is specific: the site was built with the WPBakery page builder, which was later removed. Its stored shortcodes now display as plain text with nothing to read them. That is proof this predates your work, and it is the kind of thing only someone reading the backend would ever catch.

02

Every product reads Out of Stock, with no explanation.

Across the store, every item is marked unavailable and nothing tells a visitor why or when it returns. Direct-to-consumer revenue through the site is zero right now, no matter how many people arrive ready to buy.

03

No analytics and no email tool sit behind at least five signup surfaces.

Five different places invite a visitor to sign up, including a popup that promises 10 percent off. No system behind the site can actually send that discount, and no analytics record who arrived or where they came from. The 36,000 views you generated cannot be attributed to anything, because nothing is there to catch them.

04

The homepage links to no social profiles at all.

Not one. Not even the Instagram you are actively growing. A visitor who lands on the site has no path to the audience you are building, and the audience you are building has no path back to the store.

05

Three products still say "COMING SOON JUNE 2026" in August.

Three homepage products and a Google search title carry a launch date that has already passed. The first thing a searcher sees is a promise that is now two months stale.

06

A copywriter's working note is live in the homepage copy.

Left in the published page, word for word:

lets delete "Pints arrive in an insulated box with plenty of dry ice.

One unfinished line, sitting on the live homepage. It is the small detail that tells you the read is real. You only find something like this by reading every word on the page, which is exactly what I did.

Their site promises this email. Nothing behind it can send it. So I built it.
